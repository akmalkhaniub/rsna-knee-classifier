KL Grade Clinical Status Radiological Pathological Hallmarks Attention Focus
Grade 0 Normal No radiographic features of osteoarthritis. Uniform Joint Space
Grade 1 Doubtful Minute osteophytes of doubtful clinical significance. Incipient Margin
Grade 2 Minimal Definite osteophytes with unimpaired joint space. Lateral Femur
Grade 3 Moderate Multiple osteophytes, definite joint space narrowing. Medial Compartment
Grade 4 Severe Large osteophytes, severe sclerosis, bone-on-bone deformity. Complete Collapse
